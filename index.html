Product Case Studies

Case Study Snapmint, BNPL Conversion

Low BNPL Conversion Was a Trust Problem, Not a UX Gap

The Signal

Low first-order conversion at the credit check.

The Real Problem

Trust and fear among first-time credit users in Tier 2/3 India.

What I Proposed
Social proof before credit checks, likely limits before document requests, and total-cost framing from the first screen. As a moonshot, passive UPI-based pre-approval.

Case Study FitTrack, First-Week Churn

First-Week Churn Was a Motivation Problem, Not a Tracking Problem

The Signal

30% uninstalled in week one; the brief asked only for onboarding fixes.

The Real Problem

Beginners wanted motivation rather than tracking.

What I Proposed
Micro-habits with daily streaks, after pushing back on the onboarding-only brief.
How It's Measured
7-day activity.
